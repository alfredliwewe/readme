This system is a comprehensive, easy-to-use platform designed to streamline and enhance school administration in Malawi. Developed to meet the specific needs of Malawian secondary schools, this system aims to simplify tasks for teachers, administrators, and students alike, creating a more organized and efficient environment for learning and growth.

The system covers a wide range of essential school management tasks, including student records, exam results management, fee payments, and teacher assignments. By automating these processes, it minimizes paperwork, reduces the risk of errors, and ensures easy access to critical information when needed. This solution empowers schools to focus more on delivering quality education while efficiently handling the administrative aspects of school operations.

Whether you are a principal, teacher, administrator, or IT manager, this documentation will guide you through the setup, features, and functionalities of the Secondary School Management System to help you make the most of this tool in managing your school.

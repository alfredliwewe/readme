# Managing Students
As the superadmin in the Secondary School Management System, the head teacher plays a crucial role in overseeing and managing student records. This responsibility is central to maintaining an organized and up-to-date database that supports student development and school administration. The head teacher’s student management tasks include:

## 1.Adding Students: 
The head teacher can add new students to the system, inputting essential details such as student personal information, and parents or guardian. This process helps ensure that each new student is accurately registered and can access resources relevant to their class and year. 

_Image below shows how to add new student by filling the form_
![Add Students Dialog](https://malawi-schools.com/readme/uploads/images/add_students_modal.png)

## 2.Importing Student Data: 
For faster data management, the head teacher has the option to import student records in bulk. This feature is especially useful at the start of a new school term, when several new students might be joining. By importing data, the head teacher can save time, ensure data consistency, and quickly populate the system with essential student information.

To do that, create the excel document with the following structure. __`please note that the structure is mandatory`__
![Add Students Dialog](https://malawi-schools.com/readme/uploads/images/students_import_structure.png)

After finishing entering data, save the file as __*CSV Comma Delimited*__ file. `Standard .xlsx file will not work!!`

On the system choose __Import Students__, you will be given a dialog, there choose your file. Preview it and confirm import.

## 3.Manage Parents: 
The head teacher can also link students to their parents or guardians within the system. This includes adding and updating parent contact information, which ensures effective communication between the school and parents regarding student progress, events, and other important updates.

Normally parents accounts are created from students parent information, and they get automatically assigned to the student as their guardian. But otherwise, other parents can be created under __parents tab__

From there you can go to the __students tab__, `edit`, then search the parent name, and assign as their child.
![Add Students Dialog](https://malawi-schools.com/readme/uploads/images/choose_parent.png)

## 4.Deactivating Students: 
When students leave the school due to graduation, transfer, or other reasons, the head teacher can deactivate their accounts. Deactivation keeps records intact for future reference without crowding the active student list, ensuring that only currently enrolled students are visible in relevant sections of the system.

_Deactivating students is different from deleting them (__of which you can delete too, where necessary__)_. Deactivation will keep student information such as subject registration and grades for future use. `Deleting them will entiry remove the students and their data`

### Conclusion
By managing these aspects of student records, the head teacher ensures the system remains an accurate and comprehensive database that supports both school administration and student success. This oversight promotes smoother communication with parents, keeps records organized, and maintains up-to-date information on each student's status.
